# Melon

Mobile browser drop-and-merge game: `index.html` (HTML/CSS + game-over overlay) + ES modules in `js/`. Paper cutout garden theme, seed → melon growth chain. An original game in the drop-and-merge genre: keep its own theme, art and chain, and don't copy any specific existing game.

## Architecture

- HTML5 Canvas 2D, fixed-step 60Hz sim (`STEP`) driven by `requestAnimationFrame`, DPR capped at 2
- World is a fixed 400×760 unit space, letterboxed to the screen (`view` in `js/render.js`, `toWorld()` for input)
- `js/constants.js`: world/jar geometry, danger line, timings, spawn weights, scoring bonuses (`MELON_BONUS`, `TRICK_*`), HUD positions
- `js/items.js`: `TIERS` (name, radius, points, color, optional scalloped silhouette, draw fn). Sprites are drawn in unit space (radius 1) into offscreen canvases by `buildSprite()`, rebuilt on resize at device resolution. Each sprite has a cream paper rim and layered pieces with small drop shadows, then a grain overlay.
- `js/paper.js`: seeded RNG (`mulberry32`), wobbly outlines (`blob`, `ellipsePts`, `rectPts`), smooth tracing, shared grain texture
- `js/physics.js`: **must stay deterministic across browsers**: only `+ - * /`, `sqrt`, `min/max` (no `Math.hypot`, `**`/`pow`, trig, or `Math.random`). `resetPhysics()`/`getLastH()` expose the slow-mo step memory for saves and replays. Verlet circles, 8 substeps × 2 collision passes. Velocity is implicit (`x - px`, per substep). When the step size changes (slow-mo), velocities are rescaled. Mass = R². Same-tier contact calls `onMerge` (the game creates the merged body). Merged bodies grow from the old radius with easeOutBack. Anti-launch guards: `MAX_SEP` caps push-apart speed at contacts, and `MAX_UP` caps upward speed. Without them, squeezed items and stacked shockwaves throw pieces out of the jar. Each body records its hardest impact per frame (`hit`, `hnx/hny`) for the juice layer. Each body also keeps a flight clock, updated per substep: `air` (seconds since it last touched a fruit or the floor; walls don't count) and `lx` (its x at that touch), plus a `touch` flag the game reads and clears once a frame. `shockwave()` pushes neighbours on merge and reports each one to `onPush`. Spin is visual only (a no-slip rolling estimate).
- `js/game.js`: state `s`, input (pointer events plus keyboard), drop cooldown, overflow rule (a body older than `OVERFLOW_GRACE` with its top above `DANGER_Y` for `OVERFLOW_LIMIT` seconds → game over), save/resume via localStorage (`melon_save_v2`: full float state of `SIM_KEYS` + `BODY_KEYS`, saved on drop, every 3s, and on hide). Runs are deterministic:
  - spawns come from a seeded mulberry32 whose state lives in `s.rng`; `s.tick` counts `update()` calls; each drop logs `[tick, x in tenths]` to `s.log` and the score at that moment to `s.marks`. Anything that moves bodies must depend only on these (visual randomness uses `Math.random` and must not touch the sim)
  - replay (`watch(run)`, phase `'replay'`): re-runs the log from the seed at ~30s total (×4 while the player was idle), SFX silenced via `setQuiet`, no haptics/discoveries; tap skips. A drift prints `replay drifted` to the console
  - challenge links: `#vs=<encodeRun(run)>` starts a race on that seed; the HUD shows your lead vs their score at the same drop count; the game-over card offers replay / challenge (`navigator.share`, else clipboard) / watch theirs. Player name (`melon_name`) is auto-generated and editable on the card
- Juice (game.js):
  - impacts → squash spring (`sq/sqV/sqA` per body), landing "tok" sounds (max 3/frame, 0.15s per-body cooldown), dust, shake for big pieces
  - merges → hit-stop (`s.freeze`), slow-mo for tier ≥7 (`s.slowT`; sim dt scaled), shockwave, themed particles, flash, camera zoom kick, haptics
  - combo: merges within `comboWindow` (1.4s) stack a ×0.25 multiplier (cap ×4). Combo 5 starts **fever** (8s, extended +0.6s per merge, ×2 points, 2.2s combo window, rainbow visuals, faster/denser music)
  - trickshot (merge → hop → merge → merge): a merge arms the merged piece and every piece its shockwave pushes (`arm()`, `TRICK_POP` s to break contact; a piece already airborne restarts its flight clock at the push). Once it leaves contact it's a flight until it touches a fruit or the floor (bank shots off walls count). A flight of ≥`TRICK_AIR` s and ≥`TRICK_DX` units sideways, averaging ≥`TRICK_VX` units/s sideways, that merges with its twin starts a cascade (`chain` 1 on the new piece); each new piece has `TRICK_LINK` s (`linkT`) to merge again (chain + 1). The 2nd merge is the trickshot: bonus `(TRICK_BONUS + 2× new tier's points) × mult` (the API mirrors this in its ceiling), "TRICKSHOT!" banner (y 300), hit-stop, bullet time (`s.bullet`, 0.2× sim speed), the reveal camera (punch in on the merge, then `frame()` the whole path), whistle SFX, and a shooting-star trace (`js/trace.js`) that runs the full path and leaves a fading echo of the line. One per settle: a trickshot sets `s.trickLock`, and `settle()` clears it once every piece has stayed under `TRICK_STILL` units/s for `TRICK_SETTLE` s; while locked, further cascade merges only extend the star's path (no bonus), so dropping matching pieces onto a cascade can't farm points. `pop/fly/air/lx/chain/linkT` are sim state (in `BODY_KEYS`), `bullet/trickLock/calmT` are in `SIM_KEYS`; paths (`b.path`, `b.tpath`, a point a frame) are visual only. `s.tricks` counts shots and the game-over card shows it. Bot calibration: about 1 trickshot per 11 random-bot games, ~200 points each
  - danger: heartbeat SFX, music low-pass, red vignette, trembling pieces and held item
- `js/render.js`: **perf model** — rendering is fill-rate bound, not JS bound. The canvas covers only the letterboxed world; the margins are a static CSS background with the same grain. DPR is capped at 2 and by `MAX_PIXELS` (2.6M). `adaptQuality()` lowers `view.quality` if frames stay over 21ms. Background (color, glow, grain) is cached once per resize and blitted. Avoid new full-screen gradient or scaled-image passes: fever is a solid `fillRect` wash, the danger vignette is 4 edge bands, the flash is a cached texture, and the merge pulse is a ring stroke. Particles are capped at `MAX_PARTS` (360) and only big ones get a shadow. Measure with headless Chrome at 1920×1080×2. Background glow pulsing on the music beat (`beatPhase()`) and hue-cycling in fever; camera (`js/camera.js`: eased focus/zoom for moments like trickshots, kept inside the world) plus shake and zoom kick; the HUD and banners are drawn outside the camera; jar; aim guide; bodies (batched shadow pass, motion trails, squash via `drawItem(..., sq, sqA)`); held item (pops in, tilts/stretches with aim speed); effects and the trickshot trace; HUD drawn on canvas (score punch, combo/fever meters, next, mute/restart, growth-chain strip); screen-space danger vignette and melon whiteout
- `js/fx.js`: tier-themed bits (`BITS`: seeds, leaves, petals, chunks, confetti) with flutter, additive sparkles and flashes, rings, background color pulses, floating text, banners (fixed y slots: combo 262, trickshot 300, fever/melon 330–340, discovery 405), ambient pollen motes pushed by merges, confetti rain
- `js/audio.js`: Web Audio synth through a compressor. SFX: drop whoosh, landing tok, merge bloop+bell (pitch rises with tier and combo), sub thump for big pieces, fever riser, trickshot whistle, heartbeat. Generative music (100 BPM, ×1.25 in fever, C pentatonic): pad, bass, soft kick/hats, random-walk marimba. `setMood({fever, danger})` adds layers or closes a low-pass. One mute toggle for everything.
- `js/haptics.js`: `navigator.vibrate` (Android); iOS 18+ Safari via the hidden `<input switch>` label-click trick. iOS likely only honours it inside user gestures (drop / buttons), so merge haptics may be silent there.
- Theme: `THEMES.light/dark` palettes in `js/render.js`, swapped by `setTheme()` (rebuilds the cached background, sets `data-theme` on `<html>` for the CSS overlay vars). Toggled by a bare moon/sun icon under the restart button (`BUTTONS.theme`). Defaults to the system preference until the player picks one; an inline head script applies it before first paint. Item sprites keep their colors; only the scissors rim around each one takes the theme (`pal.rim`), so `setTheme()` rebuilds the sprites too.
- `js/run.js`: run packing for links (varints → base64url, ~5 bytes/drop), `decodeRun` validates and returns null on junk; random garden names
- `js/net.js`: scores API client (`API` URL constant). Anonymous per-device `melon_id`; game over submits the run (`s.lastRun.rank` → "#3 today · #41 all time" on the card); failed sends queue in `melon_pending` and retry on load. The card's 🏆 board opens a today / all-time overlay; tapping a row fetches that run and replays it
- `api/`: Flask scores API on Railway (project `melon-scores`, service `api` + Postgres; https://api-production-e5b59.up.railway.app). Deploy with `cd api && railway up -s api -d`. Stores every run's full log (`runs`), one name per player (`players`); boards show each player's best. "Today" is the UTC date. Checks: field validation, ≥26 ticks between drops, score ceiling from the seed's own spawn sequence plus a per-drop trickshot allowance (`spawns()` mirrors the game's RNG; keep it in sync with `SPAWN_WEIGHTS`/`roll()`), 40 posts/hour/IP. `POST /api/admin/delete {player|run}` with `Authorization: Bearer $ADMIN_TOKEN` (Railway variable on `api`) for moderation. CORS allows erickfm.github.io and localhost:8000
- `js/es.js` + `js/es-lessons.js`: **Spanish mode** (opt-in, off by default; bare "ñ" icon `BUTTONS.lang`, red when on), built for a beginner living at home with a native speaker. UI strings use `L(en, es)` inline (static DOM uses `data-es`). In play the voice (`speechSynthesis`, prefers es-MX/es-US/es-419) counts combos in Spanish and cheers big merges with everyday praise; plant names are shown but never spoken. After each run of 10+ drops, the next of 40 everyday dialogues (`DIALOGUES`: morning, coffee, meals, cooking, garden, walks, errands, plans, feelings, his family), each with one key phrase. Her lines use feminine forms. Chat-style bubbles; tap a word for its gloss (tap it again or 🔊 to hear it; nothing speaks on the first tap), tap a speaker tag to hear the line, 🔊 plays the dialogue in two voices (male for him, female for her; pitch split if only one voice), "inglés" shows per-line translations, ‹ › flip back through the phrasebook. Every word form must be in `GLOSS` and every key phrase must appear in its dialogue (headless check). `melon_es` has a version; bumping it resets progress
- Font: **Mali** (Google Fonts, 400–700) everywhere: hand-drawn with real bold and full Spanish glyphs. Canvas sizes in the code were tuned for Gaegu, so `font()` multiplies by `FONT_SCALE` (0.8); CSS sizes are already scaled. Banners shrink to fit 340 units wide
- localStorage keys: `melon_best`, `melon_discovered` (highest tier ever seen, reveals the chain strip), `melon_muted`, `melon_dark`, `melon_name`, `melon_save_v2`, `melon_id`, `melon_pending`, `melon_es` (`{v, on, next, looked}`)
- Debug: `window.__melon` exposes `{ s, bodies, drop, TIERS, fx, makeBody, update, newGame, watch, replayStep, save, load, encodeRun, decodeRun }` for headless checks (e.g. play a bot game with `update(STEP)`, then `watch(s.lastRun)` and assert the bodies match bit-for-bit)

## Running

```bash
python3 server.py   # http://localhost:8000
```

Deployed on GitHub Pages from `main` (repo root, `.nojekyll`), live at https://erickfm.github.io/melon/. Pushing to `main` redeploys. Keep asset paths relative: the site lives under `/melon/`.

`server.py` sends `Cache-Control: no-cache` so iOS Safari revalidates modules.

## User Preferences

- Mobile-first (iPhone Safari), portrait
- Warm, handmade paper cutout aesthetic (same family as the slasher project)
- Iterative development, tune numbers rather than removing mechanics
